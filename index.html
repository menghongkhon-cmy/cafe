Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I order from my table?

Scan the QR code on your table card or select your table number on our floor plan. Add items to cart and checkout as dine-in.

Can I reserve a specific table?

Yes! Choose your preferred zone when booking. We'll assign the best matching table and confirm by phone.

How long is my reservation held?

We hold your table for 15 minutes after your reservation time. Call us if you're running late.

Is there a waitlist on busy days?

When all tables are occupied, join the waitlist in-store or on this page. We'll text when your table is ready.

Do you offer split bills?

Ask your server or use the "Request Bill" button. We can split by item or equally among guests.

Are pets allowed on the patio?

Well-behaved pets are welcome at outdoor tables T-16 through T-20. Water bowls available on request.
